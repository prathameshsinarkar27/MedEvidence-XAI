# Retrieval benchmark (Phase 7)

Small **synthetic** benchmark: 6 invented documents (`documents/`, generated by `scripts/generate_retrieval_benchmark.py`),
44 queries with hand-written relevance judgments (`queries.json`, split into 22 `dev` and 22 `test`), and the last measured
results (`results.json`). No real patient data. Judgments were written from the documents' text, not from system output.

Run: `python scripts/evaluate_retrieval.py` (dev only) or `python scripts/evaluate_retrieval.py --final` (also the held-out test split).
Method, results and limits: `docs/evaluation-retrieval.md`.
